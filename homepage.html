Questions buyers ask first

Fast answers for a refrigerator that is already leaking cold air.

The homepage should answer the urgent questions before the customer has to dig through product lists.

How long does it take?

Stock items can ship the same day. Custom-made gaskets usually take 3-15 days depending on size and availability.

How do I measure?

Measure the outside edge of the gasket, photograph each door, and include the nameplate. We can guide the final check.

Can I install it myself?

Most replacement gaskets are DIY friendly. Warm water softening and corner shaping help the gasket seal properly.

What if I might be wrong?

We manually review uncertain matches before production, especially old models, discontinued units, and missing part numbers.
